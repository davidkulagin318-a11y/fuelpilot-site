The best gas apps for road trips, and what each one is for

November 25, 2026 · By David Kulagin, maker of FuelPilot

No single app does everything on a road trip. Use GasBuddy or iExit to see prices, Upside to get cash back at the pump, Kibi to pick exits with food and clean bathrooms, Roadtrippers to plan the sights, and FuelPilot to plan every fuel stop from your tank and drive you there. Here's what each is best at, so you can pick two or three that cover you.

I make FuelPilot, so weigh that. Prices and features are as listed on each app's store page or website in November 2026.

FuelPilot: plans the fuel stops for you

Most gas apps show you prices and leave the math to you: how far you can get on this tank, which cheap station you can actually reach, and whether a detour is worth it. FuelPilot does that part. It knows your tank size, learns your real MPG from your fill-ups and checks prices along the whole route. With Pro, Autopilot picks the cheapest stations you can reach for the trip, keeping the reserve you choose, and adds them to your turn-by-turn directions. Towing mode adjusts your range when you hitch up a trailer.

Good to know: prices come from HERE Technologies, not users. There's no CarPlay yet and no discount card. Navigation and the cheapest gas near you are free; Autopilot needs Pro.

GasBuddy: the biggest price map

GasBuddy's users report prices as they fill up, so it covers most towns well. Pay with GasBuddy is a payment card that takes money off each gallon, and its Plus and Premium plans save 20¢ a gallon on your first 50 gallons each month. If you want to know what every station in town charges, start here. FuelPilot vs GasBuddy goes into more detail.

iExit: what's at the next exit

iExit follows you down the interstate and shows what's at each exit ahead: gas, food, hotels, rest areas, EV chargers and RV or truck parking. It shows the cheapest gas at each exit, so you can compare the exits coming up. It works with CarPlay.

Upside: cash back on the fill-up

Upside is a free app that gives cash back at participating gas stations, grocery stores and restaurants. Claim an offer in the app before you pay, then pay as usual with your card. Gas offers can reach 25¢ a gallon, but they vary by station and day. It works only in the US.

Kibi: exits with everything you need

Kibi plans stops where you can get gas, food and a clean bathroom at one exit, timed to your car's fuel range with a safety buffer. It scores more than 79,000 US highway exits, then hands your stops to Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze. It's iPhone only, and it describes itself as not a turn-by-turn app and not a gas price tracker. If a good bathroom matters more than saving 20¢ a gallon, Kibi is a strong pick.

Roadtrippers: plan the trip, not just the fuel

Roadtrippers is a route planner for finding attractions, campgrounds and places to stay along the way. It has a free tier and paid plans with extras like offline maps and RV tools. Use it to decide where you're going and what you'll see. It doesn't plan fuel stops around your tank.

Google Maps and Waze

Free, familiar and good at traffic. Both let you search for gas stations along your route and show prices for many of them. Neither knows how far your tank will go, so picking the stop is still up to you.

A good road trip setup

  • Long trip, want it cheap and planned: FuelPilot, plus Upside for cash back where there's an offer.
  • Family trip with kids: Kibi for food and bathroom stops, and GasBuddy or iExit for prices.
  • Towing a camper: FuelPilot with towing mode, and Roadtrippers for campgrounds. See how to plan fuel stops when towing.
  • Short trip, free only: Google Maps or Waze, and GasBuddy.

Questions

What is the best gas app for a road trip?

It depends on the job. GasBuddy has the widest price coverage, Upside gives cash back at participating stations, iExit shows the cheapest gas at each exit ahead, and FuelPilot plans every fuel stop for the trip from your tank and real MPG and gives you directions there.

Is there an app that plans gas stops for a road trip?

Yes. FuelPilot plans fuel stops at the cheapest stations you can reach with your range and reserve, and adds them to its turn-by-turn directions. Kibi plans exits with gas, food and a clean bathroom around your fuel range and hands them to your maps app.

Can I use GasBuddy and Upside together?

They're separate apps, so you can have both. Use GasBuddy to compare prices and Upside to see which nearby stations have a cash back offer. Whether a GasBuddy card discount and an Upside offer both apply to the same fill-up depends on each app's terms, so check before you count on it.

Do Google Maps and Waze show gas prices?

Both show prices for many stations when you search for gas along your route. Neither plans stops around how far your tank will go.
