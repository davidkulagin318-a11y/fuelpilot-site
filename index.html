Navigation that plans your fuel stops.

FuelPilot keeps an eye on your range, checks fuel prices along your route and takes you to the cheapest stop you can reach. Made for diesel pickups, RVs and anyone who logs miles for work.

Coming soon to the App Store Free to download. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial.

How a drive works

  1. 1

    Pick a destination

    Search for a place or drop a pin. You get turn-by-turn directions with spoken guidance.

  2. 2

    FuelPilot watches your range

    It knows your tank size and MPG, or reads the real fuel level from an OBD-II adapter.

  3. 3

    It picks the fuel stop

    Before you run low, it finds the cheapest station on your route that you can reach with your reserve left, and adds it to the trip.

  4. 4

    The trip logs itself

    Miles, time, fuel and stops go into your logbook, ready for your records or your accountant.

What's in the app

Fuel Autopilot

Live prices along your whole route and the cheapest stop you can reach, picked before you need it. You choose your reserve and how much a detour has to save.

Turn-by-turn navigation

Spoken directions, rerouting and weather on the map. Directions keep going with the screen off or while you're in another app.

Cheapest gas near you

One tap finds the lowest price within the distance you set, from 1 to 20 miles.

A logbook that writes itself

Every navigated drive is saved with its route map, miles, time, MPG and stops. Tag trips business or personal and add notes.

Receipts read for you

Take a photo of the receipt and the gallons, price and total fill themselves in. Prefer to type them? Turn photo reading off in Settings.

Ready for tax time

Export a mileage log, a fuel expense report or a quarterly IFTA summary with miles and gallons by state, as a spreadsheet file.

OBD-II live data

Plug in a Bluetooth adapter for the real fuel level and MPG from your engine. It also reads and clears check engine codes.

Every vehicle, towing included

Each truck gets its own tank size and MPG, and towing mode adjusts the numbers when there's a trailer behind you.
