What is FuelPilot?

FuelPilot is a turn-by-turn navigation app for iPhone and Android that plans your fuel stops. It knows your tank size, learns your real MPG from your fill-ups and checks fuel prices along your whole route, then picks the cheapest stations you can reach before you run low. Every trip you navigate is logged with its miles and fuel, ready for taxes and IFTA.

How a drive works

  1. 1

    Pick a destination

    Search for a place or drop a pin, then choose between routes. You get turn-by-turn directions with spoken guidance.

  2. 2

    FuelPilot knows your range

    It knows your tank size and learns your real MPG from your fill-ups, so it always knows how far you can go.

  3. 3

    It plans every fuel stop

    Before you leave, Autopilot picks the cheapest stations you can reach for the whole trip. After each fill-up it checks that the next stop still makes sense.

  4. 4

    The trip logs itself

    Miles, time, fuel and stops go into your logbook, ready for your records or your accountant.

What's in the app

Navigation, fuel prices and your trip records in one place. Features marked Pro come with FuelPilot Pro. Big Rig mode comes with FuelPilot Big Rig.

Fuel Autopilot Pro

Every fuel stop for the trip, planned up front from prices along your whole route. You choose your reserve, how far out of the way you'll go and how much a detour has to save.

Turn-by-turn navigation

Spoken directions, rerouting and weather on the map. Avoid tolls or highways, and find a restroom, food, a rest area or a truck stop in one tap.

Cheapest gas near you

Tap Get gas to find the lowest price within the distance you set, from 1 to 20 miles.

The price you actually pay

Enter your fuel discounts once and every pick plans on your price. Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's and cardlock stations only show up if you have a membership.

Learns your real MPG

FuelPilot works out your real MPG from the gallons you put in and the miles you drove, so your range keeps getting more accurate. Nothing to plug in.

A logbook that writes itself

Every navigated drive is saved with its route map, miles, time, MPG, stops and what you saved on fuel. Tag trips business or personal and add notes.

Receipts read for you Pro

Take a photo of the receipt and the station, gallons, price and total fill themselves in. Prefer to type them? Turn photo reading off in Settings.

Ready for tax time Pro

Export a mileage log, a fuel expense report or a quarterly IFTA summary with miles and gallons by state, as a spreadsheet file.

Every vehicle, towing included Pro

Each vehicle keeps its own tank size and MPG, and towing mode adjusts your range when there's a trailer behind you.

Big Rig mode Big Rig

For semis, hot shots and RVs. The road-ahead list shows your fuel stops, truck stops, weigh stations, rest areas and weather and road alerts coming up. Switch it off any time to drive a car.

Questions

More answers on the support page.

Is there an app that plans fuel stops for a whole road trip?

Yes. FuelPilot's Autopilot plans every fuel stop for the trip before you leave. It picks the cheapest stations you can reach with your reserve left, on or close to your route, and adds them to your directions. After each fill-up it checks that the next stop still makes sense. Autopilot comes with FuelPilot Pro. With a free account you still see fuel prices along your route.

What's a good app for planning fuel stops when towing a trailer?

FuelPilot is built for it. Towing cuts your MPG and your range, so with towing mode on FuelPilot adjusts your range for the trailer and plans stops you can actually reach. You choose how much fuel to keep in reserve and how far out of the way you'll go for a cheaper price. Towing mode comes with Pro. See FuelPilot for towing and RVs.

Does FuelPilot do truck routing for semis, hot shots and RVs?

Yes, with FuelPilot Big Rig. Enter your rig's height, weight and length and it plans truck-legal routes around low bridges, weight limits and restricted roads. For a semi or hot shot it only sends you to truck stops and truck lanes, at the truck-lane diesel price. Truck routes depend on map data and the sizes you enter, so always follow posted signs and use your own judgment. Big Rig is on iPhone now and comes to Android in the next update. See FuelPilot for truckers.

How is FuelPilot different from GasBuddy?

GasBuddy is best known for gas prices reported by its users, the GasBuddy card discount and a trip cost calculator. FuelPilot is a navigation app: it gives you turn-by-turn directions, plans where to stop based on your tank and range, adds those stops to your route and logs the trip. Many drivers use a price app and a maps app together. FuelPilot puts both jobs in one. See FuelPilot vs GasBuddy.

How is FuelPilot different from Trucker Path?

Trucker Path is made for semi drivers, with truck stop parking availability, weigh station status and a large trucker community. FuelPilot is built around fuel planning and trip records, and works for cars, pickups and RVs as well as trucks. With Big Rig you can switch truck routing off and drive your personal vehicle in the same app. See Trucker Path alternatives.

Can FuelPilot track my mileage for taxes or IFTA?

Yes. Every drive you navigate with FuelPilot is saved to your logbook with its route, miles, time and fuel, and you can tag trips business or personal. With Pro you can export a mileage log, a fuel expense report or a quarterly IFTA summary with miles and gallons by state, as a spreadsheet. Drives you don't navigate with FuelPilot aren't logged. See FuelPilot as a mileage log.

Is FuelPilot free?

Yes. Navigation, the cheapest gas near you and a 7-day trip logbook are free, and a free account adds fuel prices along your route. Pro ($7.99 a month or $49.99 a year) adds Autopilot fuel stops, receipt reading, the full logbook with exports, and more than one vehicle with towing mode. Big Rig ($19.99 a month or $199.99 a year) adds truck routing. Both start with a 7-day free trial.

Do I need an OBD-II adapter or anything plugged in?

No. FuelPilot plans from your tank size, the fuel level you set and the MPG it learns from your fill-ups.

Does FuelPilot work in Canada?

Yes. FuelPilot works in the United States and Canada.

Does FuelPilot work with Apple CarPlay or Android Auto?

Not yet. CarPlay is built and will be turned on in a later update.
