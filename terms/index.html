Drive safely

  • Set your route before you drive, rely on the spoken directions, and pull over or let a passenger handle the phone if you need to change something.
  • Directions, traffic, road conditions and weather can be wrong or out of date. Always follow traffic laws, road signs and directions from police and road crews.
  • Routes are planned for passenger vehicles. FuelPilot doesn't check bridge heights, weight limits, truck or hazardous materials restrictions, or roads closed to trailers and RVs. If your vehicle is tall, heavy or towing, check the route yourself.
  • Range, fuel level and MPG are estimates. Don't let your tank run lower than you're comfortable with because the app says you'll make it.

Fuel prices and stations

Fuel prices and station details come from outside providers and can be out of date or wrong. A station may be closed, may not sell the fuel you need, or may not have room for your vehicle. The price on the pump is the price you pay. Savings shown in the app are estimates based on the prices we had at the time.

OBD-II adapters

Use an adapter that's compatible with FuelPilot, follow its maker's instructions, and plug it in or unplug it only while parked. Not every vehicle reports every reading, and readings can be missing or wrong. Clearing check engine codes turns the light off but doesn't fix what caused it, and it can reset the readiness monitors that an emissions inspection checks. We aren't responsible for adapters made by other companies.
