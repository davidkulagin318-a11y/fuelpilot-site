Fuel stop planning for towing and RVs

A trailer can cut your MPG by a third or more, so the range you're used to is wrong the moment you hitch up. FuelPilot plans your fuel stops around the range you really have while towing, picks the cheapest station you can reach, and gives you turn-by-turn directions there.

How it works when you tow

  • Towing mode adjusts your range. Each vehicle keeps its own tank size and MPG, and with towing mode on FuelPilot adjusts your range for the trailer behind you.
  • It learns your real MPG. FuelPilot works out your MPG from the gallons you put in and the miles you drove, so your range gets more accurate with every fill-up. Nothing to plug in.
  • Autopilot plans every stop. Before you leave, it picks the cheapest stations you can reach for the whole trip, keeping the reserve you choose. After each fill-up it checks that the next stop still makes sense.
  • You set the rules. Choose how much fuel to keep in reserve, how far out of the way you'll go and how much a detour has to save before it's worth it.
  • The price you actually pay. Enter your fuel discounts once. Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's and cardlock stations only show up if you have a membership.

Motorhomes and big fifth wheels

With FuelPilot Big Rig, set your vehicle type to RV / motorhome and enter its height, weight and length. Routes then clear your rig's size, avoiding low bridges, weight limits and restricted roads, and every fuel station still shows so you can pick the one that suits you. Truck routes depend on map data and the sizes you enter, so always follow posted signs and use your own judgment. Big Rig is on iPhone now and comes to Android in the next update.

Questions

How far can I tow on a tank of fuel?

Multiply your usable tank size by your towing MPG, then keep a reserve. A 36-gallon tank at 9 MPG towing is 324 miles, and keeping a quarter tank in reserve leaves about 243 miles between stops. Towing MPG varies with weight, wind, hills and speed, which is why FuelPilot learns your real MPG from your fill-ups instead of using the window sticker.

What's the best app for planning fuel stops while towing a travel trailer?

Look for an app that plans stops from your towing range, not just one that lists prices nearby. FuelPilot does that: it adjusts your range for the trailer, picks the cheapest stations you can reach along the route and adds them to your turn-by-turn directions.

Will FuelPilot route my motorhome around low bridges?

Yes, with FuelPilot Big Rig and your RV's height, weight and length entered. Without Big Rig, routes are planned for a passenger vehicle and don't account for bridge heights or weight limits, and the app tells you the route hasn't been checked for your vehicle's size.

Does FuelPilot show diesel prices?

Yes. FuelPilot shows prices for the fuel your vehicle uses, including diesel, from HERE Technologies. Prices can lag behind the pump, so the price on the pump is the one you pay.

Can I plan a trip with FuelPilot before I leave?

Yes. Pick a destination and choose between routes. With Pro, Autopilot shows every planned fuel stop in the route preview, and you tap Add stops to add them.

Does it work for a diesel pickup that isn't towing?

Yes. Each vehicle keeps its own settings, so you can plan with your normal range when the trailer's off and switch towing mode on when you hitch up.
