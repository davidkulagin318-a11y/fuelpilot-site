How a drive works

  1. 1

    Pick a destination

    Search for a place or drop a pin, then choose between routes. You get turn-by-turn directions with spoken guidance.

  2. 2

    FuelPilot knows your range

    It knows your tank size and learns your real MPG from your fill-ups, so it always knows how far you can go.

  3. 3

    It plans every fuel stop

    Before you leave, Autopilot picks the cheapest stations you can reach for the whole trip. After each fill-up it checks that the next stop still makes sense.

  4. 4

    The trip logs itself

    Miles, time, fuel and stops go into your logbook, ready for your records or your accountant.

What's in the app

Navigation, fuel prices and your trip records in one place. Features marked Pro come with FuelPilot Pro.

Fuel Autopilot Pro

Every fuel stop for the trip, planned up front from prices along your whole route. You choose your reserve, how far out of the way you'll go and how much a detour has to save.

Turn-by-turn navigation

Spoken directions, rerouting and weather on the map. Avoid tolls or highways, and find a restroom, food, a rest area or a truck stop in one tap.

Cheapest gas near you

Tap Get gas to find the lowest price within the distance you set, from 1 to 20 miles.

The price you actually pay

Enter your fuel discounts once and every pick plans on your price. Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's and cardlock stations only show up if you have a membership.

Learns your real MPG

FuelPilot works out your real MPG from the gallons you put in and the miles you drove, so your range keeps getting more accurate. Nothing to plug in.

A logbook that writes itself

Every navigated drive is saved with its route map, miles, time, MPG, stops and what you saved on fuel. Tag trips business or personal and add notes.

Receipts read for you Pro

Take a photo of the receipt and the station, gallons, price and total fill themselves in. Prefer to type them? Turn photo reading off in Settings.

Ready for tax time Pro

Export a mileage log, a fuel expense report or a quarterly IFTA summary with miles and gallons by state, as a spreadsheet file.

Every vehicle, towing included Pro

Each vehicle keeps its own tank size and MPG, and towing mode adjusts your range when there's a trailer behind you.
