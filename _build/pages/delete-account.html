Delete your FuelPilot account

You can delete your FuelPilot account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open FuelPilot and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and it can't be undone.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@fuelpilotapp.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request is yours, delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

Everything stored with your account:

  • your account, email address, display name and profile photo
  • your vehicles and settings
  • your trips, including their routes, notes and tags
  • your receipts and receipt photos
  • any exported reports

What's kept

Apple or Google, and RevenueCat, our subscription service, keep records of FuelPilot Pro purchases as needed for billing, refunds and tax. We don't keep any other copy of your data.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel FuelPilot Pro. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings first; the support page shows how.
