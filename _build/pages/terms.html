4. Routes, clearances and Big Rig

  • Unless Big Rig mode is on, routes are planned for passenger vehicles. FuelPilot then doesn't check bridge heights, weight limits, truck or hazardous materials restrictions, or roads closed to trailers and RVs. If your vehicle is tall, heavy, long or towing, check the route yourself.
  • With Big Rig mode on, routes are planned for the vehicle type and size you enter, using map and restriction data from outside providers. That data can be incomplete, wrong or out of date. Bridges, clearances, weight limits and restrictions change because of construction, resurfacing, snow, ice and new signs, and the data may not show those changes.
  • A route is only as good as the height, weight, length, axle, trailer and cargo details you give it. You're responsible for entering them correctly and keeping them up to date.
  • A truck route or "Route clears your RV" result isn't a promise that a road, bridge, turn, ramp, parking area or station is legal, safe or physically passable for your vehicle. Check clearances and restrictions yourself, follow posted signs, and follow the rules that apply to your vehicle, your license and your load, including hours-of-service, permit and hazardous materials rules.
  • If the app says Not a truck route, Not checked for your RV or Car route — not checked, the route hasn't been checked for your vehicle's size.
  • Weigh station status, rest areas, parking, drive and break timers are information only. The drive clock is not an electronic logging device and doesn't replace one.
  • FuelPilot isn't a commercial routing or dispatch service, and it isn't designed or certified for oversize or overweight loads, hazardous materials compliance, or emergency use.

5. Fuel prices and stations

Fuel prices and station details come from outside providers and from drivers' reports, and can be out of date or wrong. The price on the pump is the price you pay. Savings, discounts and trip costs shown in the app are estimates.
