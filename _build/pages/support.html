Getting started

What do I need to set up first?

Allow location when FuelPilot asks, then add your vehicle in the Vehicle tab with its tank size and MPG. Range and fuel stop picks depend on those two numbers, and FuelPilot fine-tunes your MPG as you log fuel-ups.

Is there an Android version?

FuelPilot is launching on iPhone first. Android is coming after that.

Fuel prices and Autopilot

Where do the fuel prices come from?

From HERE Technologies, a fuel price provider, and they're refreshed regularly. Prices can still lag behind the pump, so the price on the pump is the one you pay.

Why didn't Autopilot pick the cheapest station I can see?

Autopilot picks the cheapest station you can reach with your reserve left, on or close to your route. A cheaper station might be out of range, too far off the route, or not save enough to be worth the detour. You can change your reserve and the minimum savings for a detour in Settings › Autopilot.

Why doesn't it send me to Costco or a cardlock?

Stations that need a membership are left out until you tell FuelPilot you have one. Turn on Costco, Sam's Club, BJ's or fleet cardlock in Settings › Fuel memberships.

Navigation

Does FuelPilot avoid low bridges and truck restrictions?

No. Routes are planned for passenger vehicles and don't account for height or weight limits, truck or hazardous materials restrictions, or roads closed to trailers. If you're tall, heavy or towing, check your route and follow posted signs.

Directions stop when my screen locks.

On your iPhone, open Settings › FuelPilot › Location, choose Always and turn on Precise Location. FuelPilot only uses your location in the background while you're navigating.

The spoken directions sound robotic.

FuelPilot uses the best voice installed on your iPhone. To download a better one, open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, pick English and download an Enhanced or Premium voice. Then choose it in FuelPilot's Settings.

Trips and exports

How are trips recorded?

Every drive you navigate with FuelPilot is saved to your logbook automatically. The free plan shows the last 7 days. Older trips are kept, and they all come back if you upgrade to Pro.

How do I export a mileage log or IFTA report?

In the Trips tab, tap Export, choose a mileage log, fuel expense report or IFTA summary, and pick the period. You get a spreadsheet (CSV) file and a private download link that works for 24 hours, so you can email it or open it on a computer. Exports are part of FuelPilot Pro.

How does the IFTA summary work out miles by state?

From the GPS path of the trips you navigated with FuelPilot, checked against official state boundaries, and fuel by state from where your receipts were scanned. Drives you didn't navigate with FuelPilot aren't included, so check the totals before you file.

Receipts

How do I log a fuel-up?

Tap Add receipt and take a photo of the receipt. With FuelPilot Pro, the station, gallons, price and total are read from the photo for you to check and confirm. You can always type the details in yourself.

Can I stop receipt photos being read by AI?

Yes. Turn off Read receipts with AI in Settings › Receipts. Photos are then only stored with your receipt, and you enter the numbers yourself.

FuelPilot Pro

What does Pro include, and how much is it?

Fuel Autopilot with prices along your whole route, receipt reading, your full logbook with exports, and more than one vehicle with towing mode. It's $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year in the US, and starts with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, tap FuelPilot and then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel the subscription.

I got a new phone and Pro is gone.

Sign in to FuelPilot, open Settings › Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure your phone is signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds for the App Store. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the email we send you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings › Profile › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all its data. If you can't sign in, see Delete your account.
