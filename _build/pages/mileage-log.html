A mileage log that fills itself in

Every drive you navigate with FuelPilot is saved to your logbook with its route, miles, time and fuel. Tag it business or personal, add a note, and export a mileage log or fuel expense report when tax time comes. FuelPilot also plans your fuel stops at the cheapest station you can reach, so the trip costs less in the first place.

What gets logged

  • Every navigated drive. The route map, miles, time, MPG, stops and what you saved on fuel, saved automatically when you drive with FuelPilot's directions.
  • Business or personal. Tag each trip and add notes, such as the client or job it was for.
  • Fuel receipts. Photograph a receipt and, with Pro, the station, gallons, price and total are read for you. You can always type them in yourself.
  • More than one vehicle. With Pro, add every vehicle you drive, each with its own tank size and MPG.

Exports

With FuelPilot Pro, open the Trips tab, tap Export and choose a mileage log, a fuel expense report or a quarterly IFTA summary for the period you need. You get a spreadsheet (CSV) file and a private download link that works for 24 hours, so you can email it to your accountant or open it on a computer.

What it costs

The logbook is free for your last 7 days. Older trips are kept, and they all come back if you upgrade. Pro is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year in the US, starts with a 7-day free trial, and adds the full logbook, exports, receipt reading and Fuel Autopilot. FuelPilot works in the United States and Canada.

Questions

Does FuelPilot track my mileage automatically?

It logs every drive you navigate with FuelPilot, with no extra taps. It doesn't record drives you make without starting directions in the app, so start a route for each work trip you want in the log.

Can I export a mileage log for my taxes?

Yes, with FuelPilot Pro. Export a mileage log or a fuel expense report as a spreadsheet for any period and send it to your accountant. Ask your tax preparer what your records need to show for your situation.

What's a good mileage tracker app that also saves money on gas?

FuelPilot does both: it logs the drives you navigate for your records, and its Autopilot plans each fuel stop at the cheapest station you can reach on your route.

Does it work for gig and delivery drivers?

Yes, for the drives you navigate with FuelPilot. Tag each one business or personal and export the log when you need it.

Can it do IFTA for a hot shot or semi?

Yes. The quarterly IFTA summary has miles and gallons by state, worked out from the GPS path of the trips you navigated and the receipts you scanned. See FuelPilot for truckers.
