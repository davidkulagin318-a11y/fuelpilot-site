Trucker Path alternatives: free and paid truck GPS and fuel apps compared

November 25, 2026 · Updated November 27, 2026 · By David Kulagin, maker of FuelPilot

Trucker Path is the app most drivers already have, and its Diamond plan plans fuel stops too. If you're an owner-operator paying for your own fuel, the questions are what that costs, what else you need, and whether another app does the job for less. Here are the main alternatives, what each is best at, and where Trucker Path is still the better pick.

I make FuelPilot, one of the apps on this list, so weigh that. Prices are US prices as listed on each company's site or the App Store in November 2026. They change, so check before you buy.

First: what Trucker Path already does

The free app covers truck stops, fuel prices, parking, weigh station status history, a load board and a big driver community. The Diamond plan, $29.99 a month, $149.99 for six months or $249.99 a year with a 7-day trial, adds CarPlay, trips with more than three stops, HOS rest reminders and Smart Fuel Plans, which work out where to stop and how much to put in. Any user can also buy discounted fuel through the app.

So if you need parking and a fuel plan in one app and the price is fine with you, Trucker Path Diamond may be all you need. The alternatives below make sense when you want something cheaper, something free, or something that also covers your pickup, your RV or your IFTA miles.

FuelPilot Big Rig

Best for: owner-operators and hot shot drivers who want every fuel stop planned from their real range, and their IFTA miles worked out from the trips they drive.

  • Truck-legal routes for your rig's height, weight and length, plus axles, trailers and hazmat.
  • Fuel Autopilot picks the cheapest truck stop or truck lane you can reach for every stop on the run, before you leave, and re-checks after each fill-up. It plans at the truck-lane diesel price.
  • It learns your real MPG from your fill-ups. No OBD-II adapter or ELD connection needed.
  • A quarterly IFTA summary with miles and gallons by state, from the GPS path of the trips you navigated and the receipts you scanned. Drives you don't navigate with FuelPilot aren't included.
  • A road-ahead list of your fuel stops, truck stops, weigh stations, rest areas, and weather and road alerts.
  • Switch Big Rig off and it's a normal navigation app for your car or pickup.

Where it falls short: it shows weigh stations on your route but not whether they're open, and there's no parking availability, no CarPlay yet and no load board. Big Rig is on iPhone now and comes to Android in the next update. It's $10 a month less than Trucker Path Diamond and $50 a year less on the annual plan.

Hammer

Best for: drivers who want a truck GPS and don't want to pay for one.

Hammer is a free truck GPS from TruckersReport.com, with offline maps and lane guidance, covering the US, Canada and Mexico. If all you need is truck-safe directions, start here. You'll still be picking your own fuel stops.

CoPilot Truck

Best for: drivers who run through areas with no signal and want navigation that works offline.

CoPilot is a long-running truck navigation app with offline maps. Truck navigation is a subscription, $9.99 a month or $119.99 a year in the US App Store, with a 14-day trial.

Mudflap

Best for: cutting what you pay per gallon.

Mudflap isn't really a navigation app. It's a free app for discounted diesel at more than 3,600 truck stops, with savings that vary by stop and can reach $1.00 a gallon. There's no credit check, and paying by debit card or bank account has no fee. It also has free truck-safe navigation. Mudflap pairs well with a planning app: find out where you need to stop, then check whether a discounted stop is close.

TruckMap

Best for: a free look at what's ahead.

TruckMap is a free truck GPS that shows diesel stations, weigh stations and parking. It's a good free backup to whatever you use as your main app.

Sygic Truck & RV

Best for: drivers who want offline truck navigation, including outside North America.

Sygic is a truck and RV GPS with offline maps and routing for your vehicle's size. Truck navigation is a paid subscription; check the App Store for current prices. Like CoPilot and Hammer, it gets you there safely but leaves picking fuel stops to you.

How to choose

  • Parking is your biggest problem: stay with Trucker Path. Parking availability and its driver community are what it does best.
  • Fuel is your biggest cost: use an app that plans stops from your range, like FuelPilot Big Rig or Trucker Path Diamond, and add Mudflap for discounts.
  • You don't want to pay: Hammer for directions, Mudflap for discounts, TruckMap for stops and parking.
  • You also drive a car or pull an RV: FuelPilot covers both in one app.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Trucker Path?

It depends on what you use Trucker Path for. For fuel planning and IFTA miles at a lower price, FuelPilot Big Rig. For a free truck GPS, Hammer or TruckMap. For offline navigation, CoPilot Truck or Sygic Truck & RV. For diesel discounts, Mudflap. For parking, Trucker Path is still the one to beat.

Does Trucker Path plan fuel stops?

Yes, with a Diamond membership. Smart Fuel Plans use your truck's fuel settings and prices along your route to pick stops and how much fuel to add at each one.

Is there a cheaper truck GPS that plans fuel stops?

FuelPilot Big Rig is $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It plans truck-legal routes for your rig's size and picks the cheapest truck stops and truck lanes you can reach along the way.

Is there a free truck GPS app?

Yes. Hammer and TruckMap are free truck GPS apps, and Mudflap includes free truck-safe navigation alongside its diesel discounts.

Which truck app works for hot shots?

Any truck GPS that lets you enter your rig's height, weight and length. FuelPilot Big Rig has a Hot shot / HD pickup vehicle type, sends you to truck stops and truck lanes and plans from your real MPG.
