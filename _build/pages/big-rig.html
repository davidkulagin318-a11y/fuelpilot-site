Truck GPS that plans your fuel stops

FuelPilot Big Rig plans truck-legal routes for your rig's height, weight and length, sends you only to truck stops and truck lanes at the truck-lane diesel price, and picks the cheapest stop you can reach. Every trip is logged with miles by state for IFTA. For semis, hot shots and RVs.

What Big Rig does

  • Truck-legal routes. Enter your rig's height, weight and length, plus axles, trailers and hazmat. Routes avoid low bridges, weight limits, restricted roads and hazmat restrictions. Any box you leave blank uses a typical semi: 13'6", 80,000 lb and 72 ft.
  • Truck stops and truck lanes only. For a semi or hot shot, fuel stops are truck stops and truck lanes, priced at the truck-lane diesel price.
  • Fuel Autopilot. Every fuel stop for the run, picked before you leave from prices along the whole route and the range you really have. After each fill-up it checks the next stop still makes sense.
  • The road ahead. A list of what's coming up on your route: your fuel stops, truck stops, weigh stations, rest areas, restrictions, and weather and road alerts. Weigh stations on your route also show on the map.
  • IFTA summary. Export a quarterly summary with miles and gallons by state, worked out from the GPS path of the trips you navigated and the receipts you scanned.
  • Receipts read for you. Photograph a receipt and the station, gallons, price and total fill themselves in.
  • One app for the truck and the car. Switch Big Rig mode off on the map to get car routes and every gas station, and back on for the truck.

Truck routes depend on map data and the sizes you enter, so always follow posted signs. If truck routing can't be reached, FuelPilot shows the car route and warns you before you start and while you drive. If no legal truck route exists for a trip, it tells you instead of routing.

Questions

Is there a truck GPS app that also plans fuel stops?

Yes. FuelPilot Big Rig combines truck-legal routing with fuel planning: it picks the cheapest truck stops and truck lanes you can reach along a truck-legal route, adds them to your directions and logs the miles for IFTA.

Does FuelPilot work for hot shot trucks?

Yes. Set your vehicle type to Hot shot / HD pickup and enter its height, weight and length. Routes follow truck-legal roads and fuel stops are truck stops and truck lanes at the truck-lane diesel price.

Can FuelPilot do my IFTA miles by state?

It works them out for you. Miles by state come from the GPS path of the trips you navigated with FuelPilot, checked against official state boundaries, and fuel by state comes from where your receipts were scanned. Export the quarterly summary as a spreadsheet. Drives you didn't navigate with FuelPilot aren't included, so check the totals before you file.

Does it avoid hazmat-restricted roads?

Yes. Mark your load as hazmat in your vehicle settings and Big Rig routes avoid hazmat restrictions.

Does FuelPilot show weigh stations?

Yes, with Big Rig. Weigh stations on your route show on the map and in the road-ahead list, along with your fuel stops, truck stops, rest areas, and weather and road alerts. It doesn't show whether a scale is open right now.

Can I use it in my own car on the weekend?

Yes. Turn off the Big Rig switch on the map or in Settings and you get car routes and every gas station again.
